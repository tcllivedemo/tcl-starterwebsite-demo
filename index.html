About the business

A small local team that treats your home like our own.

Amihan started in 2018 as a two-man team riding around Molino with a pressure washer and a borrowed tricycle. Eight years later we're a crew of six trained technicians, but the way we work hasn't changed: we arrive on time, lay down our own drop cloths, explain what's actually wrong with your unit, and quote the price before we start.

We specialise in residential and small-business units — homes, clinics, salons, sari-sari stores and small offices. If a unit can still be saved, we'll tell you. If it can't, we'll say that too.
